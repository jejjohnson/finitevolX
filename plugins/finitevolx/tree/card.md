- **Provides.** Finite-volume building blocks for ocean and atmosphere models on Arakawa C-grids: grids with a ghost ring, difference, interpolation and advection operators, land masks, boundary conditions, Poisson and Helmholtz solvers, and time steppers.
- **Start from.** `gridax.CartesianGrid2D`, `gridax.Difference2D`, `gridax.Interpolation2D`, `gridax.Advection2D`, `gridax.Mask2D`, `gridax.enforce_periodic`, `gridax.solvers.solve_helmholtz_dst`; time steps are `integrix.march.rk3_ssp_step`.
- **Keep.** Arrays include one ghost ring and operators write only the interior, so fill the ghosts of every field before each operator reads it, including inside the right-hand side of a time stepper; build a `Mask2D` once and pass it as `mask=`.
- **Never.** Write shifted-slice differences or hand-copied ghost cells; use the operator whose name says the stagger mapping, such as `diff_x_T_to_U` or `U_to_T`, and a boundary-condition fill.
- **Open the skill when.** You need spherical grids, WENO or TVD scheme choices, multilayer or vertical-mode code, masked or multigrid solvers, the Laplacian as a linear operator, or diffrax and IMEX time stepping.
